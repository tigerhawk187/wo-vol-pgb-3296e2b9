How to read this

Intake = opportunities received through the Commercial toll-gate (HubSpot deals created since Jan 1, 2025). Transferred to Lift = closed-won deals that became actionable field work. Win rate (resolved) = won ÷ (won + lost), excluding deals still open in HubSpot (i.e. not yet won or lost).

Data notes

Sources: CATL from Renewance Lift (Quickbase). Sungrow, Risen, LG from HubSpot deal creation. Refreshed 27 Jul 2026.
Methodology: CATL counts work orders by catl_submittingtime (CATL’s real intake timestamp), falling back to appointment time, then Lift created date for the 4% of records missing both. This replaces the prior “Date Created in Lift” basis, which produced spurious spikes when records were bulk-loaded. Other OEMs count HubSpot deals by created date. LG figures exclude 28 NextEra placeholder deals to reflect real intake (23 since Jan 2025).
Cadence: auto-refresh every Monday ~2:03 AM Central.
